Trading Experience

Markets Are
Just Systems

I approach Forex and cryptocurrency markets through the same lens I use for systems analysis: find the rules, understand the edge cases, and execute with discipline. Markets reward analytical thinking and punish emotional decision-making.

My trading is built on technical analysis, market structure reading, and risk management. I don't predict — I wait for high-probability setups and manage position size so losses are survivable and profits compound.

Trading and cybersecurity share a core: both require patience, pattern recognition, and the ability to act on incomplete information while controlling risk.
