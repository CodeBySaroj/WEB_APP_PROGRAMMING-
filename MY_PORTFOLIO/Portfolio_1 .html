I am an undergraduate Computer Engineering student at Tribhuvan University with a focused mind and a quiet intensity. I do not chase noise — I study patterns, understand systems, and build mental models before I act.

"The most powerful skill is not coding or trading — it is the ability to learn any system faster than others."

My interest spans two worlds that share the same core discipline: cybersecurity and financial markets. Both reward patience, analytical rigor, and the ability to think several steps ahead. Both punish impulsiveness.

In technology, I gravitate toward low-level systems, Linux internals, and security research. In finance, I study market structure, price action, and risk management with the same methodical approach. I am self-taught in most of what I know, and I prefer depth over breadth.

trading experience

Markets as a System

I approach Forex and cryptocurrency markets the same way I approach systems in computer engineering: understand the rules, find the edge cases, and operate with strict logic rather than emotion.

Trading taught me that discipline is the most valuable variable. A brilliant strategy without risk management is a vulnerability. A mediocre strategy with strict execution and loss control outperforms anything emotional.

I focus on technical analysis, market structure, and the psychology of market participants. My edge comes not from prediction, but from reading setups where probability tilts in my favor and position sizing ensures I survive the losses.

Market Analysis

Technical analysis, price action reading, market structure identification, and multi-timeframe confluence.

Strategy Development

Building rule-based systems with defined entry, exit, and invalidation criteria. No guessing.

Risk Management

Position sizing, max drawdown limits, and capital preservation as the primary objective.

Market Psychology

Understanding crowd behavior, sentiment cycles, and how emotion creates exploitable patterns.
